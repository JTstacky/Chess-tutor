    SPARK HEIST by Teng GamesSmall creations. Fair fights. Big ideas.

    YOUR FIRST HEIST

    Bring the glowing Sparks home.

    1. Invent: type an idea or pick a picture. Check its cost and weakness.
    2. Build: select a team slot, then use your invention. Spend up to six Ink.
    3. Plan: spread runners across lanes. Fetch collects; Intercept disrupts; Escort protects.
    4. Clash: a creature needs a brief peaceful moment to pick up a Spark. Hits interrupt it. Protect your runners!
    5. Command: halfway through, give one creation a new job and speed burst.
    6. Adapt: bank the most Sparks to win the round. First to three round points wins.

    Every round resets health and Ink. No permanent power upgrades. A draw gives half a point each.
