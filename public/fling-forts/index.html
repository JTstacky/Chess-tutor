How to play

Sit side by side, tablet in landscape. Each player owns one fort.

  • Fling: drag back on your side and let go. A longer pull throws harder; pull upward to shoot down.
  • Win a round: destroy the enemy castle. Its chief shields it while standing, so knock him out first. Each knockout keeps him down 6 s longer.
  • Shots: Normal is free. Scatter splits in three, Flame burns the ground, Explosive smashes buildings. Some races roll a shot through troops.
  • Power: every level makes all your shots hit harder (up to 5 a round).
  • Troops: tap one and it marches out.
  • Build: one tap. Walls block, towers shoot, mines earn gold (smash an enemy mine for 50). Shield domes your castle; the next one waits 30 s.
  • Hold a button to see what it does.
  • Crates: pop a balloon, then hit the landed crate to claim it.
  • Timing: new shots, buildings and gold unlock at 1 and 2 minutes. At 4 minutes comes sudden death: shields fall, castles and chiefs take double damage. Win two rounds.
  • Modes: Frenzy (no reload, 2× gold, 2× speed), Rapid fire (no reload), Gold rush (2× gold), Turbo (2× speed). Online is Classic.
  • Races each have two strengths and two weaknesses, shown when you pick.
