Fling Forts

Two forts. One tablet. Fling, build, march.

How to play

Sit side by side with the tablet in landscape. The left player owns the left fort, the right player the right one.

  • Fling: drag back anywhere on your side and let go, like a slingshot. Longer pull, harder throw. Only the start of the flight is shown.
  • Win a round: knock out the other chief. Smash the roof and drop one in, or break down the front wall.
  • Ammo: Stone is free. Scatter splits in three, Firepot burns the ground, Boulder smashes blocks.
  • Troops: tap a troop and they march out. They fight in the middle, then batter the fort.
  • Build: one tap builds. Wall, Tower and Mine go up at once in the build strip in front of your fort. A Mine gives gold every second (up to two). A Vault gives gold too: one only, built on your fort. Shield puts a dome over your fort for a while.
  • What is it? Hold your finger on any button to see its name, its class (Melee, Ranged, Anti-air, Siege and so on) and what it does.
  • Bonuses fly across the sky. Hit a kite and it is yours. Pop a balloon and its crate drops: the first troop to reach it gets it.
  • After 80 seconds comes sudden death: shields fall and chiefs take double damage. Win two rounds.
