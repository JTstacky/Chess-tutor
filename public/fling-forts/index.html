Fling Forts

Two forts. One tablet. Fling, build, march.

How to play

Sit side by side with the tablet in landscape. The left player owns the left fort, the right player the right one.

  • Fling: drag back anywhere on your side and let go, like a slingshot. Longer pull, harder throw. Pull upward to shoot down toward the ground. Only the start of the flight is shown.
  • Win a round: destroy the other castle. Its chief guards it: while he is standing the castle takes no damage, so knock him out first. He gets back up after a while, but each knockout adds six seconds to his next recovery, even across rounds. Hit the castle hard while he is down.
  • Fling shots: the Normal shot is free. Scatter splits in three, Flame burns the ground, Explosive has the biggest blast (best against castles, walls and towers). Some races also get the Rolling shot: it lands, then rolls on, crushing troops in its path.
  • Power: the Power button makes all your fling shots hit harder, up to five levels a round. Each level costs more than the last.
  • Troops: tap a troop and they march out. They fight in the middle, then batter the fort.
  • Build: one tap builds. Wall, Tower and Mine go up at once in front of your castle. A Mine gives gold every second (up to two), and smashing an enemy mine pays 50 gold. Shield puts a dome over your castle for a while.
  • What is it? Hold your finger on any button to see its name, its class (Melee, Ranged, Anti-air, Siege and so on) and what it does.
  • Bonuses: pop a balloon to drop its crate. Wait for it to land, then hit the crate with a fling shot to claim it. Troops and chiefs cannot collect it. Crates above castles drift to open ground; either player can win the second shot.
  • Stages: a round starts slow. New shots, buildings and gold come in at 1 and 2 minutes.
  • After 4 minutes comes sudden death: shields fall, and castles and chiefs take double damage. Win two rounds.
  • Races: every race has two strengths and two weaknesses (a stronger castle, a faster reload, a tough chief, cheaper troops...). They are on the race card when you pick.
