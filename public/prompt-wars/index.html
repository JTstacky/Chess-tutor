Invent, seal, battle

Both players submit a private opening prompt before Start fight unlocks. Invent a turtle fortress, feather ninja, ice wizard or dragon garden. Combine a light, balanced or heavy body with bolt, scatter or beam and shield, cooler or booster. During combat, open a new private invention to adapt.

Creative constraints

Conjure wall cover, ice, fire or a repair garden. Repair gardens appear near their inventor; other fields appear between robots. Fields affect both sides, last 12 seconds, cost 30 energy and have an 8-second cooldown; at most two per player. Walls cut shots through them by 65%. Light bodies are faster and fragile; heavy bodies are slower and armored. AI maps imaginative requests into these mechanics and explains limits. Blitz / Standard / Epic give 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 game tokens; Standard opening text is capped at 300. Hidden instructions and bundled art are free.
