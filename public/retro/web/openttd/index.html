Transport Tycoon Deluxe

OpenTTD 15.3 with the free OpenGFX graphics and OpenSFX sounds, in your browser.

Tap to click; drag the map to scroll (with a build tool selected, dragging builds); two fingers pan and pinch to zoom; hold a finger for a right click (tooltips). Mouse: right-drag scrolls, wheel zooms, F3 opens the save window (loading is in the main menu and the toolbar's disk menu), Esc closes windows, Tab is fast forward.

Engine: OpenTTD by the OpenTTD team (see credits), version 15.3, in our web build. Licensed under the GNU GPL v2; the build's full source: openttd-retro source (tar.gz).
