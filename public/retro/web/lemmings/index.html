    Controls

    Mouse: pick a skill on the panel, click a lemming to give it the skill. Drag or move to the screen edge to scroll. Double-click the mushroom to nuke.
    Keys: F1/F2 release rate, F3–F10 or 1–8 skills, P or F11 pause, F12 nuke (press twice), F fast forward, arrows scroll, Esc levels.
    Touch: tap a skill, then tap near a lemming (the box shows who got it). Drag to scroll. Tap the nuke twice to confirm.
