Keys: arrows move (up jumps, down ducks, diagonals jump or duck forward and back), Enter punches, right Shift kicks. Enter also confirms in menus; Esc goes back. Player 2 (two player game): W A S D with Q E Z C diagonals, left Ctrl punches, left Shift kicks.

Engine: OpenOMF by Tuomas Virtanen, Andrew Thompson, Hunter and the OpenOMF contributors, under the MIT licence (our web fork's source: openomf-retro source (tar.gz)). Music plays through libxmp 4.6.3 (LGPL-2.1-or-later); networking code links ENet (MIT) and the settings file is read with libConfuse 3.3 (ISC). Sound through SDL2 and SDL2_mixer (zlib).
