Keys: arrows move, Shift+arrow shoots, T torch, P pause, S save, Esc or Q quit. On the title screen: P play, W pick a world, R restore a game, A about, H high scores.

Engine: our web build of libzoo by Adrian Siekierka (asiekierka), a C re-implementation of the Reconstruction of ZZT, both MIT licensed; our fork adds the web frontend, ZZT's own title screen, prompts and high scores.

Game: ZZT (1991) and Super ZZT (1992) are © Epic MegaGames, Inc. (Tim Sweeney). Epic's licence allows them to be copied and distributed at no cost in their original form; they are not public domain and are only served to signed-in accounts.
