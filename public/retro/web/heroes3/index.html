Game files

Choose your Heroes III Complete folder, a ZIP, or its game files. Full accounts can also load the shared files.

On a computer, you can also drop the folder, files, or ZIP here.

Game files stay on this device. Online games send only the current save to the private turn relay.

Where are the files?

GOG owners: usually C:\GOG Games\HoMM 3 Complete or C:\Program Files (x86)\GOG Galaxy\Games\HoMM 3 Complete.

CD owners: usually C:\Program Files (x86)\3DO\Heroes 3 Complete or C:\Program Files (x86)\3DO\Heroes3.

Choose the whole folder if possible. On a phone or iPad, choose the files inside Data, Maps, and Mp3, or make a ZIP on a computer.

Saved games
